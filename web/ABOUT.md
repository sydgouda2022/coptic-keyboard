# Source and linguistic scope

This standalone keyboard uses the Coptic surface catalog compiled from the pinned
KELLIA Comprehensive Coptic Lexicon v1.2 (2020), Coptic SCRIPTORIUM forms, and
published KELLIA supplement. The master has 20,652 distinct surfaces (12,246
Sahidic-only, 3,358 Bohairic-only, 4,041 both, 1,007 unassigned).

This is *not* a complete inventory of historical Coptic and does not implement a
morphological or next-word language model. Predictions are source-listed prefix
matches with optional locally learned ranking. An item being listed does not
prove a specific manuscript witness or lemma identity.

Editorial dictionary display notations (parentheses, leading stars, phrases,
compounds with punctuation) stay in the preserved source master but are **not**
offered as literal single-word insertions. An unassigned dialect is never
silently classified as Sahidic or Bohairic. The complete, unchanged source
inventory is in `release/`, with its SQLite evidence and source hashes.

Only the plain-text draft, chosen dialect and local learning counts are saved
in this device's browser storage. Nothing is uploaded. Your browser may clear
storage; use **Export .txt** for a durable backup.

The KELLIA, SCRIPTORIUM and supplement datasets retain their respective
attribution and redistribution requirements; refer to their original licences.
The application code is distinct from those datasets.
