# Sources and limitations

Coptic Unicode completion uses source-listed Sahidic/Bohairic written forms from
the pinned KELLIA Comprehensive Coptic Lexicon v1.2 (2020), Coptic SCRIPTORIUM
seed forms and KELLIA published supplements. The archival release contains
20,652 unique written surfaces and 38,194 SQLite spelling records; it is not a
historically exhaustive Coptic vocabulary.

Source-listed suggestions do **not** prove a specific manuscript witness or
a unique lexical identity. Prefix search and local learning are not a
morphological or sentence-level language model. Editorial annotations,
parentheses, stars and multiword expressions are preserved in the source master
but not suggested as literal insertable words.

The app stores its draft, chosen dialect and personal ranking in this
browser's local storage. No accounts, servers or telemetry. Browser storage
can be cleared: export a plain-text copy for a durable backup.

The lexicon's source hashes and provenance are recorded in the repository's
`release/` directory and the original KELLIA/SCRIPTORIUM editions. The
respective source licences and attribution obligations remain applicable.
